Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to FTI Studio. We respect your privacy and want to protect your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit our website and use our desktop application.

2. Data We Collect

Because FTI Studio is a lightweight desktop image editor, we do not require user accounts and we do not collect personal information, tracking metrics, or your edited image files. Your data stays entirely local to your device.

3. Cookies and Third Parties

Our official site is hosted via GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect server logs and basic technical data (such as your IP address) for security and operational purposes. We do not place tracking cookies or share data with third-party advertisers.

4. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your technical data under laws like the GDPR or CCPA. Since we do not store personal data, we cannot access, modify, or delete any personal records.
